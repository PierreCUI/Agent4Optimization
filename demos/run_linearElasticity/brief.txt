Linear elastic cantilever topology optimization on a rectangle of two by one. The left edge is clamped, and a vertical downward surface load acts on a short segment at the middle of the right edge. The solid material has Young's modulus 200 and Poisson's ratio 0.3, and the void region is softened by a factor of one hundred. The objective minimizes the material volume, weighted by 0.25, plus the structural compliance.
