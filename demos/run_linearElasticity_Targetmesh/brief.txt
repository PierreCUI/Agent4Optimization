Linear elastic target-deformation topology optimization on a coarser mesh of the cantilever domain: the objective measures the signed distance of the deformed material to a prescribed disk, restricted to the inner region. The left edge is clamped with a very stiff spring, and a downward body load acts on the inner region. The solid has Young's modulus 2000 and Poisson's ratio 0.3, with the void softened by a factor of one hundred.
