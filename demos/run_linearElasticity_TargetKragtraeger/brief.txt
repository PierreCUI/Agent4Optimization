Linear elastic target-deformation topology optimization in which the objective tracks a quadratic circular target evaluated in the deformed configuration, restricted to the inner region by a second characteristic function. The structure is clamped on the left edge with a very stiff spring, and a downward body load acts on the inner region. The solid has Young's modulus 2000 and Poisson's ratio 0.3, with the void softened by a factor of one hundred.
