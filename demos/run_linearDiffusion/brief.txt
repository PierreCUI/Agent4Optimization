Linear diffusion topology optimization on the unit square with a notch on the left edge. The design is encoded by a level-set function: the solid region has unit conductivity, and the void region has a weak conductivity of one tenth. The objective combines a penalty on the material volume, weighted by a Lagrange multiplier of 100, with the dissipated thermal energy. A volumetric heat source of minus ten acts inside the domain. The temperature is fixed at zero on the notch boundary, and homogeneous Neumann conditions hold on the remaining boundary.
