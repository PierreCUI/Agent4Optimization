Coupled thermo-elastic topology optimization on a long rectangle of four by one. Two coupled state fields are solved: the temperature, driven by heat conduction with a Robin boundary condition that exchanges heat with an ambient profile varying linearly with height, and the displacement, driven by linear elasticity with thermal expansion and a gravity body load on the material region. The objective combines the material volume with a strong spring energy on the clamped boundary and a reward for downward displacement on the loaded boundary. All material coefficients are softened by a factor of one thousand in the void region.
