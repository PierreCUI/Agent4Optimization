Nonlinear diffusion topology optimization on the unit square with a notch on the left edge. The conductivity depends nonlinearly on the temperature, growing quadratically with it. The objective combines a penalty on the material volume, weighted by 100, with the dissipated thermal energy. A volumetric heat source of minus ten acts inside the domain. The temperature is fixed at zero on the notch boundary, and homogeneous Neumann conditions hold on the remaining boundary.
