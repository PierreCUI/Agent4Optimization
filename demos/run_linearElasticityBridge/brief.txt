Linear elastic bridge topology optimization on a long rectangle of four by one. The bridge is supported at two segments of the bottom edge, where the displacement is fixed, and it carries a vertical downward surface load on the top edge. The solid has Lamé parameters one and eight, and the void region is softened by a factor of one thousand. The objective combines a penalty on the material volume, weighted by 100, with the stored elastic energy. The initial design prescribes two thin deck strips spanning between the supports.
