Nonlinear elastic cantilever topology optimization with a Neo-Hookean hyperelastic material law in a large-deformation setting. The left edge is clamped, and a vertical downward surface load acts on a segment of the right edge. The objective minimizes the material volume, weighted by 0.25, plus the stored elastic energy. The solid has Young's modulus 200 and Poisson's ratio 0.3, with the void softened by a factor of one hundred.
