Linear elastic target-deformation topology optimization on the cantilever domain: under load, the structure should deform toward a prescribed quadratic target profile evaluated in the deformed configuration. The objective is restricted to the material region by a second characteristic function. The left edge is clamped and reinforced with a very stiff spring, and a downward body load acts on the material region. The solid has Young's modulus 2000 and Poisson's ratio 0.3, with the void softened by a factor of one hundred.
